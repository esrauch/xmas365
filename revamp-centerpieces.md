# XMAS365™: Revamp (No More Centerpieces)

A new progression design, decided 2026-11-08 and not yet built. It replaces
each room's centerpiece and the Calendar Desk with **five relics per room**
and a mascot cage that opens as they come home. Once it's built, fold this
into `GAME.md`, `PUZZLES.md`, `SCRIPT.md` and `ROADMAP.md`.

## In one paragraph

Every room is an intro run, then a run on the room's **home twist**, then
**five relic runs**, one for each other holiday, each bringing that holiday's
twist into this room's genre. Solving a relic run frees the relic, and you
carry it to its own room, where it goes on a shelf under the caged mascot and
opens one of the cage's five padlocks. The fifth relic frees the mascot, who
moves to Holiday Hall. When all six are free, the Merger Contract opens. Any
beaten run can be replayed, or played forever in free play.

## What changes, at a glance

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Warm-up → 2 strays → bring 2 items home → centerpiece run → mascot freed | Intro → home twist → 5 relic runs; bring 5 relics home → mascot freed |
| 12 items (2 per holiday) | **30 relics** (5 per holiday, one in each other room) |
| A holiday's own twist appears in its centerpiece | Its own twist is the room's **home twist** run |
| Centerpiece run per room | **Gone** as a concept (the props stay as decoration) |
| Calendar Desk (replays, generators, daily "Day 366") | **Gone**: replays and free play live on each room's runs; no daily for now |
| Lamps light on restoring a centerpiece | Lamps light when the mascot is freed |
| Escalation memos per mascot freed | Every 5 relics returned |
| Contract: 29 panels per clause | **22** per clause |

## A room

### The screen

- **The bottom: the ladder of runs.** Intro, then home twist, then the five
  relic runs.
- **The top: the shelves.** Five relic shelves, one per other holiday, and
  above them the **mascot's cage shelf**.
- **An empty shelf** shows a dim outline of its relic and a small badge for
  the room the relic is stranded in, so you always know where to go.

### Runs

| Run | Panels | Boards | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intro** | 5 | 5×5 → 7×7 | The genre's rules, taught panel by panel (the old warm-up). |
| **Home twist** | 5 | 5×5 → 7×7 | The room's own holiday twist, at home (built from the old centerpiece runs). |
| **Relic runs** (×5) | 3 each | 5×5 → 7×7 | Another holiday's twist in this genre. The first panel is a tiny one-solution board that teaches the twist, since a twist can be met first in any of five rooms. |

About **23 panels a room, ~140 across the six.** 9×9 boards are only in free
play and the Contract.

The home twists, one per room:

| Room (genre) | Home twist |
|---|---|
| Halloween (Webs) | **dark**: hidden knot counts |
| Thanksgiving (Carving) | **cuts**: fences nothing may cross |
| Christmas (Lights) | **snow**: the mirror frame |
| Valentine's (Words) | **letters**: the coded letters |
| Easter (Hops) | **burrows**: hop underground between matching burrows |
| Fourth of July (Pictures) | **colours**: red and blue runs that need a gap |

With the home twist in the rooms, every pair of genre and twist (all 36)
appears in the story.

### Unlocking

- **Thanksgiving starts open**, as now.
- **A room's relic runs all open** once its home twist is beaten. Thanksgiving
  fans the game out straight away: its five relics belong to the five other
  rooms.
- **The first relic you bring to a room opens that room**, and goes straight
  onto its shelf.

### Restoring

- **Each returned relic** goes on its shelf and **opens one padlock** on the
  cage. Nothing else changes in the room until the last one.
- **The fifth relic** opens the cage. The mascot hops off to Holiday Hall, the
  cage goes, and the room bursts into its full colour and music.
- **The old centerpiece props** (the Spider's Web, Feast, Tree, Mailbox, Egg
  Tree, Grand Finale) stay as **plain room decoration**: drained while the room
  is taken over, in full colour once its mascot is free. They have no game role.

## Holiday Hall

- **The Calendar Desk is removed.** Its spot in the right foreground becomes
  **the freed mascots' corner**: each mascot appears there as it's freed, so
  the hall fills up as you go.
- **Tapping a freed mascot** plays a short line.
- **The lamps** above the doors light when that holiday's mascot is freed. Six
  lamps open the Merger Contract.
- **No daily puzzle** for now; it can come back after release.

## Free play

- **Tap any beaten rung** of a room's ladder to choose **Story levels**, or
  **Free play** at **5×5, 7×7 or 9×9**: endless generated puzzles in the
  browser (`src/gen/worker.ts`).
- **The intro rung's free play** is the plain genre with no twist. The home
  twist and each relic get their own.
- Still to decide: whether to show a solved count.

## Story and script

- **Room memos** (with the caged mascot's plea on a sticky note) arrive after
  the **intro run**, as they do now after the warm-up.
- **Escalation memos** come every 5 relics returned: E1 at 5, E2 at 10, … E6 at
  30 (all mascots free).
- **A mascot's thank-you** (`<room>.freed`) plays when its fifth relic comes
  home.
- **Santa stands in the hall during the Contract.** The script is rewritten to
  match: he no longer "bursts in" at the loophole, and the "never imagines it's
  *someone*" thread goes.
- Lines still to write: hints on the relic shelves, and the relic-return beats
  (T1–T3 now refer to relics and shelves).

## The Merger Contract

Each clause (one per genre) is **22 panels**:

1. **6 rule reminders**, one per twist (the home twist included).
2. **All 15 two-twist pairs.**
3. **1 all-twist boss.**

**132 panels** in all. The current clauses (29 panels) lose their base panel
and six solo panels.

## Saves

**Reset, not converted.** It's pre-release: bump the save version and start
fresh.
